People who have shaped how I think, learn, and work.

## Arpit

Arpit came into my life and taught me so many things. He is incredibly smart,
hardworking, and kind, all at the same time.

He understands concepts so easily, and he can explain them just as easily.
Sometimes I wonder how one person can be this smart. With the way he explains
every topic and the depth of knowledge he has, I truly believe he deserves to
be a professor. I strive to be like him.

He is also one of the kindest people I know. He keeps helping people even when
they never return it. He always encourages me to work hard and to help others,
and he taught me that life can throw so many difficulties at you, but if you
stay stubborn enough, you can still pave your own way.

## My parents
> कि लहू के थे जो रिश्ते उन्हें छोड़ के आ गए,
> सुकून आँखों के सामने था हम मुँह मोड़ के आ गए।
> और लूट रहे थे खजाने माँ-बाप की छाँव में,
> हम कौड़ियों की खातिर घर छोड़ के आ गए।
>
> *We left behind the ones bound to us by blood;*
> *peace was right before our eyes, and still we turned away.*
> *Under our parents' shelter, we were living like kings,*
> *yet for a handful of pennies, we left our home.*


My dad is the most hardworking, emotional, and sensitive person I know. From my
childhood, he taught me: *if you have only 10 rupees, give 1 rupee to someone
who needs it.* Always donate, always help people. He is so empathetic, and he
is the one who gave me my dream of building a shelter for dogs.

He is my motivator. Whenever I feel tired, I just need to talk to him, and I
feel energized again. He never listened to what people said about me; he just
kept motivating me. I know where I have come from, and I know how much my
father sacrificed for me to be here. I love him not just because he is my dad,
but because of the values he has given me. All I am is because of him.

My mom is my partner in everything. She is such a kind soul and such a hard
worker. She is the one who made sure we were always well fed, and she loved
Rio so much. I know how sad she was when he passed away.

I miss her a lot. There are so many things she did that I always took for
granted, and only now do I truly understand their value.

I love them both.
