## What I've worked on

This summer, I interned at **Otsuka** as a GenAI Engineer, helping build the
agentic AI platform the team was developing. Before that, I spent two years
as a software engineer at **TCS**.

Across those roles, I've worked in both the insurance and pharma domains.
Pharma is the one closest to my heart. Personal experiences have shown me
how much healthcare shapes people's everyday lives.

I'm also a **Part-Time Lecturer at Rutgers**, where I teach Computer
Applications for Business courses. I currently teach CS110 (Python and Excel)
and previously taught CS170 (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Excel) to
business-school students.

I didn't know I'd love teaching until I started, but I found out quickly
that I do. Some of my own professors showed me how much it matters to be an
understanding and empathetic teacher, and I try to bring that to every class.
My goal is always to be the best help I can to my students.
