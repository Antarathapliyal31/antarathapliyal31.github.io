About

Hi, I'm Antara. 👋

We are children who grew up with hunger; a day will come when we'll have everything. Now that we've left home, we won't go back without making history.

I'm an MS Computer Science student at Rutgers, and this is my scratchpad, where I write short notes on things I'm learning.

What I've worked on

This summer, I interned at Otsuka as a GenAI Engineer, helping build the agentic AI platform the team was developing. Before that, I spent two years as a software engineer at TCS.

Across those roles, I've worked in both the insurance and pharma domains. Pharma is the one closest to my heart. Personal experiences have shown me how much healthcare shapes people's everyday lives.

I'm also a Part-Time Lecturer at Rutgers, where I teach Computer Applications for Business courses. I currently teach CS110 (Python and Excel) and previously taught CS170 (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Excel) to business-school students.

I didn't know I'd love teaching until I started, but I found out quickly that I do. Some of my own professors showed me how much it matters to be an understanding and empathetic teacher, and I try to bring that to every class. My goal is always to be the best help I can to my students.

My Life Goals

  • Working hard, whatever the circumstances.
  • Building something that genuinely helps people with their health. I've seen firsthand how people, myself included, struggle with things they never had any control over. I want my work to make that a little easier.
  • Staying positive and happy, always.
  • Opening a shelter for dogs someday. 🐾
